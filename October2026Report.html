At a glance

Across the services reported this month, October saw 4,421 tickets raised and 3,897 closed. This covers all Lawfront Group (LFGR) firms including FSP, plus Nelsons — now split between its MSP (Quiss) and, since 1 October, recently migrated Nelsons users on the Lawfront in-house desk (452 tickets). Trethowans (AMS) logged 758 and closed 658.

Doing well: LFGR demand fell 4.9% on September to 2,890 tickets and the in-house desk closed 2,553, with SLA compliance across all priorities at 89.0% — while also taking on the newly migrated Nelsons users and closing 368 of their 452 tickets. 176 LFGR tickets (about 6%) were raised through the IT self-service portal.

To improve: SLA slipped 2.5 pts from 91.5% in September, and the shortfall sits in higher-priority work — P1–P3 tickets alone were 65.4% within target. First-time fix is 45.99% across 2,070 resolved tickets, well below the 85% target. At least 97 tickets have been open over 30 days and need clearing down.
