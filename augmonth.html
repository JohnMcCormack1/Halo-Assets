At a glance

Across all firms, August saw 3,984 tickets raised and 3,627 closed. The report covers all Lawfront Group (LFGR) firms plus MSP-managed Nelsons (Quiss) and Trethowans (AMS). FSP continued its roll-out into a second live month, with 164 tickets (up from 127 in July).

Doing well: throughput held up across the board — LFGR closed 2,516 of 2,680 raised (94%), Trethowans closed 763 against 922 raised, and Nelsons closed 348 against 382 raised. FSP's steady volume growth suggests the go-live is bedding in smoothly.

To improve: in-house priority (P1–P3) SLA compliance fell to 73.9%, down 14.6 points on July's 88.5% — effectively back to the ~74.5% level seen before July's high, suggesting July was the outlier rather than a new normal. First-time fix — now cross-checked against Halo's own live figure — is 48.7% (from 55.1% in July), down 6.4 points and still well below the 85% target. (Our initial CSV-derived estimate was 43.8%; see the note under Lawfront Group for why it moved.) LFGR also carries a 230-ticket backlog of tickets opened before September and still unresolved (per the live Halo dashboard) — worth a dedicated clear-down push. (Note: the export-derived Closed and Ageing figures we first calculated for LFGR were corrected against the live dashboard on 4 Sept — see note under Lawfront Group.)

Lawfront Group (LFGR)

Hubs: Lawfront, Farleys, Brachers, Slater Heelis, FJG, FSP · Teams: IT Services, IT Services Leads, Education, Infrastructure, Applications. SLA covers priority tickets (P1–P3); P4 excluded while agents are upskilled in triage (P4 included would read ~89.8%). First-time fix is scoped to the IT Services team only (Halo's own definition) — narrower than the 5-team scope used for Opened/Closed/SLA/PPPCM above, which is why its resolved-ticket base (2,004) doesn't match Opened (2,680). We initially estimated FTF from the flat export alone (43.8%, 2,495 resolved on the wrong 5-team scope); cross-checked against Halo's own live FTF widget on 4 Sept, corrected to 48.7% (2,004 resolved, matching within 8 tickets once the team scope was fixed) — the live figure likely reflects Halo's validated action-history calculation that isn't visible in the flat export, so we've adopted it. Comparison to July has now resumed. Dashboard cross-check (4 Sept): our first pass computed Closed and Ageing from the August-only export, which can't see tickets carried over from an earlier month. Cross-checked against the live Halo dashboard: Closed corrected from an initial 2,391 (same-month-cohort estimate) to 2,516 (the dashboard's "Tickets Closed last month" counter). Ageing/backlog corrected from an initial 3 to 230 (the dashboard's "Non-resolved tickets pre-current month" counter) — note this is opened-before-September, still unresolved, a slightly broader window than a strict >30-day cut, but the most accurate figure available. Opened (2,680, from the raw export, used for the firm/PPPCM breakdown below) sits within the ~2-ticket date-boundary noise of the dashboard's own counter (2,678) — consistent with prior months.
