At a glance

Across the services reported this month, October saw 4,421 tickets raised and 3,897 closed. This covers all Lawfront Group (LFGR) firms, Trethowans (AMS) and Nelsons — now split between its MSP (Quiss) and, since 1 October, recently migrated Nelsons users on the Lawfront in-house desk.

Doing well: LFGR demand fell 4.9% on September to 2,890 tickets and the in-house desk closed 2,553, while also taking on the newly migrated Nelsons users.

Self-service portal: 284 tickets were raised through the IT self-service portal — 221 incidents and 63 requests, about 9% of everything logged with the Lawfront IT desk, including migrated Nelsons users.

To improve: SLA fell at every priority compared with September: P1 57.1% (from 64.7%), P2 35.4% (from 44.0%), P3 67.0% (from 75.5%) and P4 92.4% (from 95.6%) within target. The most urgent tickets, P1 and P2, are furthest from target. First-time fix is 42.13% across 2,414 resolved tickets, well below the 85% target. At least 97 tickets have been open over 30 days and need clearing down.
